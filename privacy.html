Privacy policy

rclone-personal is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computers, to access the owner's own Google Drive.

  • Google Drive data is transferred only between Google Drive and the owner's computers.
  • No data is sent to any third party, sold, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored only in the local rclone configuration on the owner's computers.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
